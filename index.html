About Brittany

Clarity Is
an Inside Job

Speaker · Facilitator · Inclusive Leadership Strategist

Brittany Clausen built a career on a truth most leaders resist: you don't have a performance problem — you have an inner work problem.

She works with leaders, teams, and women business owners who have the résumé but feel the gap. Accomplished on the outside, running on empty underneath. Her work makes emotional intelligence practical — not a concept to understand, but a skill you use in real rooms, hard conversations, and high-stakes moments. That's when everything changes.
