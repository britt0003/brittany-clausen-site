About Brittany

Clarity Is
an Inside Job

Speaker · Facilitator · Inclusive Leadership Strategist

Brittany Clausen built her practice on one truth most people resist: growth doesn't happen when things calm down. It happens in the chaos — in the conflict, the crisis, the moment you realize the old patterns aren't working anymore.

She works with executives, entrepreneurs, and women who are building something — accomplished on paper, quietly exhausted underneath. People who keep hitting the same invisible wall no matter how many strategies, systems, or fresh starts they try. Her work turns emotional intelligence into something you can actually use: in the middle of the chaos, the hard conversation, the trigger you didn't see coming. That's where everything changes.
