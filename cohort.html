Full pricing will be shared with deposit holders before enrollment closes. We're not publishing it publicly yet — we want to talk to you first.

Cohort 3 dates are being finalized. Deposit holders will receive schedule details first and have the opportunity to confirm before the cohort opens publicly.

If after your intake form and our conversation it's clear the cohort isn't the right fit, we'll discuss next steps. We'd rather have the right people than the most people.
