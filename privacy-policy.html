Questions about this policy? Contact us at info@envisiongreatnessnow.com.

We collect information you voluntarily provide — such as your name and email address — when you sign up for our newsletter, submit a contact or inquiry form, or request a resource from this Site. Information you provide is used only for the following purposes:

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We use trusted third-party service providers to help operate this website — including email delivery, form handling, file storage, and site hosting. These providers may receive limited information (such as your email address) solely to perform their service on our behalf. We do not authorize them to use your information for their own purposes, and we share only what is necessary for each service to function.

If you subscribe to our newsletter, you will receive periodic emails related to Brittany's work and areas of expertise. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. You may also request removal by emailing info@envisiongreatnessnow.com.

We do not send unsolicited commercial email. We only contact people who have affirmatively opted in through a form on this website.

6. Data Retention

We retain your contact information for as long as it is relevant to an active business relationship or newsletter subscription. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at info@envisiongreatnessnow.com.

7. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

You have the right to:

To exercise any of these rights, email info@envisiongreatnessnow.com.
